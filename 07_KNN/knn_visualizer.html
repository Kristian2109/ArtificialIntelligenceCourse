Data and evaluation notes

Classification uses 80 training and 120 validation points. The first three examples share a wavy boundary, y = 0.5 + 0.22 sin(2πx), with independently jittered grids for training and validation. This spreads the points over the plot and gives a visible reference rule. The k example flips 15% of training labels; the metric example is clean; the noise example varies corruption. Feature scaling uses horizontal bands with uncorrupted labels.

Filled markers are training examples. Hollow diamonds are all validation examples, colored by their true class. Red crosses mark every incorrect validation prediction. Scores are calculated from exactly these points. Training accuracy includes self-matches and is optimistic. Validation points never vote or contribute to standardization. All voting is uniform; class ties use the nearest point and distance ties use point ID.

Regression uses a noisy numeric target, y = sin(0.8x) + 0.04x + ε, with independent training and validation samples. Prediction averages training targets only. RMSE = √mean((prediction − observed target)²), evaluated against the plotted noisy target values. Pairwise x midpoints define the exact step locations in the regression prediction curve.

Plots fit their axes to the available width, so metric contours may appear stretched on screen. Decision backgrounds are sampled; scores and neighbors are exact. Validation is for model selection: a real workflow also reserves an untouched test set. All data is synthetic and reproducible.
